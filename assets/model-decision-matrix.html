Model Selection · Decision Framework

Every pre-trained model is a trade-off you're choosing whether to make consciously.

A comparison of four production-grade models across NLP, computer vision, and tabular domains — sized, scored, and benchmarked, then weighed against each other on your terms.

Size, accuracy, and speed pull against each other

No model wins on all three axes at once. A larger model usually buys accuracy at the cost of memory and latency. A distilled or pruned model buys speed by giving some accuracy back. The right choice depends entirely on where the model has to run and what a wrong answer costs.

The matrix below treats that as an explicit input rather than an afterthought — move the sliders to reflect what actually matters for your deployment, and watch the ranking change.
