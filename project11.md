# The Middle Ground — Shark Tank Pitch Deck

<span class="eyebrow">Project 11 · Business Strategy & Product Design</span>

## Overview

A fully designed, interactive Shark Tank pitch for *The Middle Ground* — a direct-to-consumer brand built around a single, audacious premise: the Great Beverage Civil War between coffee and tea drinkers ends now.

The pitch covers brand identity, market opportunity, a live interactive "Diplomacy Engine" blend selector, revenue model, supply chain strategy, and a $750K funding ask at a $7.5M valuation.

## What's Inside

- **Brand & Narrative** — Positioned as a peace treaty between coffee and tea, with a distinct voice, visual identity, and trademarked product line (Treaty Blends №01–05)
- **Diplomacy Engine** — An interactive slider that generates a personalized coffee-to-tea blend ratio with named modes (Anarchy Mode → Nirvana Mode)
- **Market Analysis** — $14.4B US specialty beverage market; direct-to-consumer subscription model with 3 channels (website, Amazon, B2B)
- **Supply Chain Strategy** — Predictive demand model, direct farm-to-warehouse sourcing, and a Mug-Optimizer™ IoT integration roadmap
- **The Ask** — $750,000 for 10% equity ($7.5M valuation), with $250K already raised at a $2.5M seed valuation

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- Product strategy and go-to-market framing
- Interactive UI/UX design (HTML/CSS/JS)
- Supply chain and demand planning narrative
- Business storytelling and investor pitch structure
